## Home switch (v21 / v21b)
Home took ~3.6 s on the car: Maps came up full width, then a black re-layout when the safe zone switched on.
v21 switches the safe zone on before Maps resumes, through one persistent root shell, and skips debounce and root
calls when the zone is already as wanted: Maps in the right width at +0.66 s. v21 changed the dock default to LEFT;
v21b put it back to RIGHT because the left dock has no working strip (below).

## Tried and not used
- **Fixed left dock** (`attic/failed-or-parked/dock-left/`): a two-segment display cutout (left + right) as a runtime
  RRO gave a black screen on the car. Rolled back (hooks removed, `pm uninstall-system-updates` for launcher and
  Spotify, reboot). Lesson: one change per boot. A new design is needed; the dock stays fixed (no swipe dock).
- **VDS recreate** (`attic/failed-or-parked/vds-recreate/`): restarts scrcpy once if its virtual display is GPU-composed
  (an early-boot race). Proven manually (56 → 59 fps), not installed: the owner considers 56–59 fps not relevant
  next to the 99.5 % camera result.
- **Recorder R9** (intra-refresh, `attic/failed-or-parked/recorder9/`): the codec accepts it; not in the image.
- **Smear**: OTTO's output stream is clean; Ottocast `libiDevice` has a packet-queue drop path ("pkt queue overfull").
  `tooling/diagnostics/smearwatch.sh` logs it. Not seen in the benchmark; accepted by the owner.
